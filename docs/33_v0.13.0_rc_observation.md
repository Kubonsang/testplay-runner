## Blocking conditions

Stop stable promotion and preserve evidence for any BSOD, source or parent
mutation, semantic mismatch, security-boundary failure,
`manualRecoveryRequired: true`, cleanup `uncertain`, unknown attachment,
checksum/attestation failure, or nonrecoverable install/upgrade/uninstall
failure.

## Stable release blocker

```text
STABLE_RELEASE_BLOCKED_SIGNING_UNAVAILABLE
```

No public-trust Authenticode certificate or cloud-signing service is currently
available. `v0.13.0` must not be tagged until a non-exportable HSM/cloud-backed
public-trust signing identity and RFC 3161 timestamping pass a throwaway-binary
proof on clean Windows 11.

Even after signing is available, `vhdx-diff` remains an experimental explicit
opt-in in v0.13.0. Production readiness and generalized performance superiority
are not claimed.
